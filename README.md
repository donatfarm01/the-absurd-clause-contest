One absurd clause buried deep inside a terms-of-service agreement, 130 characters:

> In the event the user dies, their account shall pass to their eldest houseplant, subject to a ninety (90) day probationary period.

`main.py` writes the clause to `/output/entry.txt`. It reads like genuine account-succession boilerplate until the heir turns out to be a houseplant — and even the plant has to survive probation.
